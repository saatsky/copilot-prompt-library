Summarize the attached document into three sections:

## Key Points
Bullet list of the most important facts or claims.

## Decisions
Anything explicitly decided or agreed, if applicable.

## Open Questions
Anything unresolved or ambiguous that a reader should follow up on.

Keep the summary under 300 words unless the source material is very long.
